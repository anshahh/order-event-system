# Store Policies & FAQ

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within India.
- Express shipping (available at checkout for an extra fee) takes 1-2 business days.
- Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation.
- Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number to follow your package's progress.
- We currently ship within India only.

## Returns & Refunds
- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they're unused and in original packaging.
- To start a return, contact support with your order ID — we'll email a prepaid return label.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Sale/clearance items are final sale and not eligible for return, unless defective.

## Order Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as they haven't shipped yet.
- Once an order shows "Shipped" status, it can no longer be cancelled — a return must be initiated instead after delivery.
- To cancel, use the "Cancel Order" option on your order details page, or contact support with your order ID.

## Payment
- Payment is processed at checkout as part of order placement.
- If a payment fails, the order will show as "Payment Failed" and no charge is made — you can retry checkout.
- Note: this environment uses simulated payment processing for demo purposes, not a live payment gateway.

## Product Warranty
- Electronics (headphones, keyboards, monitors, webcams) carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects.
- Accessories (cables, hubs, stands) carry a 90-day warranty.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase (your order ID is sufficient).

## Account & Privacy
- Your order history and profile are private and only accessible when logged into your account.
- We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
- You can update your profile photo and details anytime from the Profile section.

## Contact Support
- For issues not covered here, contact support with your order ID (if applicable) for the fastest help.
- Support is available for order status, returns, cancellations, and general product questions.
